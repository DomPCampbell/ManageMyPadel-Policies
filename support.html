Frequently Asked Questions

Which booking platforms does the app support?

ManageMyPadel currently supports ebooking (ebookingonline.net) and Playtomic. You can add accounts from one or both platforms and the app will fetch and display all your bookings in a single list.

How do I add a venue?

Open Settings and tap Add venue. Select the platform, then enter your login credentials for that booking site. The app will verify the credentials and begin fetching your bookings.

Why can't the app see my bookings?

Pull down to refresh on the bookings screen. If bookings still don't appear, check that your credentials are correct in Settings — the booking platform may have required a password change. If the issue persists, contact us.

How does calendar sync work?

The app can add your padel bookings to your device calendar as events. Tap the calendar icon on any booking to add, update, or remove it. The app checks whether the calendar entry is still in sync each time you open a booking and will alert you if it has drifted out of date.

How do WhatsApp reminders work?

On a booking's detail screen, tap the WhatsApp icon next to any player to open a pre-filled WhatsApp message addressed to them. The message includes the date, time, and court details. The app records when you last sent a reminder to each player so you can see at a glance who has been contacted.

Are my login credentials stored securely?

Yes. Your credentials are stored using your device's secure storage and never leave your device. They are sent only to the booking platform you connected, in the same way as if you logged in yourself. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I remove a venue?

Open Settings, tap the venue you want to remove, and choose Remove. This deletes the stored credentials and removes all bookings fetched from that platform from the app's local database.

Does the app make bookings on my behalf?

The app displays and manages your existing bookings. It does not make new court reservations automatically. Any booking you see in the app was made through the booking platform directly.

How do I update or delete my data?

All data is stored locally on your device. You can remove individual venues in Settings, or uninstall the app to delete everything. No data is held on any external server.
